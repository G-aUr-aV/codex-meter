JUST THE NUMBERS

A smaller data footprint.

Meter keeps anonymous identifiers and numeric usage metrics. Your prompts, responses, code, tool output, chat titles and project paths are excluded from its ledger.

How collection works

A separate collector reads existing session logs through a read-only mount. It has no network access. The dashboard server receives only the sanitized snapshot and cannot access those logs or Codex credentials.

Your connection

The dashboard requires your passphrase. Cloudflare carries the HTTPS connection and can process login traffic and aggregate metrics at its edge. No private usage is saved for offline use in this browser.

Coverage has limits

Only retained logs from this EC2 host are included. Activity on another computer or cloud-only sessions may be missing. Internal review aliases can have an unknown underlying model and stay unpriced.

Your account

UNDERSTAND THE ESTIMATE

Transparent pricing.

USD prices are bundled and verified on 20 October 2026. They are applied to recorded model tokens for comparison, rather than reconstructing a historical invoice.

Cache reads and writes are parts of input. Reasoning is part of output. Each is counted once. Supported long-context thresholds and recorded speed tiers are applied per request.

Subscription fees, purchased credits, tool charges, image/audio pricing and regional premiums are outside this estimate. Unknown rates are left unpriced.
